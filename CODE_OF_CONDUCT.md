# Code of conduct

Cardio2 is built for people at a vulnerable moment in their lives. We expect everyone who takes part in our repositories, issues and discussions to act with the same care.

## Our standards

- Be respectful, patient and kind, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Welcome people of every background, age, ability and level of experience.
- Do not harass, insult or demean anyone, in public or in private.
- Do not post spam, advertising or off-topic content.

## No real personal or medical data

Never post real personal data or health data in issues, pull requests, discussions, screenshots or files. This includes names, contact details, discharge letters, measurements and descriptions of a real person's illness. Use invented examples instead.

## Medical emergencies

These repositories are not a medical service and nobody here can give medical advice.

**In an emergency, call 112** (the European emergency number) or your local emergency number.

## Reporting a problem

If someone breaks this code of conduct, email **[contact@cardio2.info](mailto:contact@cardio2.info?subject=Conduct)** with the subject line **Conduct**. We review every report and keep the reporter's identity confidential.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may block people who break this code of conduct.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all public spaces of the [cardio2](https://github.com/cardio2) organisation on GitHub.
