# Security policy

Thank you for helping keep Cardio2 and the people who use it safe.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please email **[contact@cardio2.info](mailto:contact@cardio2.info?subject=Security)** with the subject line **Security**.

Include, if you can:

- which repository, page or file is affected
- what the problem is and how it could be used
- steps to reproduce it
- how we can reach you for follow-up questions

**Please do not report security problems in public issues, pull requests or discussions.** Give us a fair chance to fix the problem before you share details publicly.

Please do not include real personal or health data in your report.

## What happens next

- We confirm that we received your report.
- We look into it, keep you informed and tell you when it is fixed.
- If you wish, we credit you once the fix is public.

## Scope

This policy covers the public repositories of the [cardio2](https://github.com/cardio2) organisation and the website [cardio2.info](https://cardio2.info).

Cardio2 is in development and is not yet certified as a medical device.
